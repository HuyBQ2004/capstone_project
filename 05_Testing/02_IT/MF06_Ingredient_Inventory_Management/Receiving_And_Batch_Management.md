## Feature: Receiving Session & Supplier Batch Management

### 1. General Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Receiving Session & Supplier Batch Management |
| Test requirement | Verify that `POST /api/v1/receiving-sessions` writes all 5 related tables inside a single atomic transaction (ADR-M06-5), that every server-side business gate (supplier `APPROVED` status BR-070, evidence photos BR-072, GPS geofence BR-074, rejection reason+photo BR-078, server-authoritative timestamp/device BR-073) is enforced even when the endpoint is called directly (bypassing the FE form, per CF-M06-12), that a duplicate supplier batch code is UPSERTed instead of duplicated (ADR-M06-5), and that the supplier-batch DB-level CHECK/FK constraints (BR-071: expiry-after-manufacture, packaging-after-manufacture, product FK, cross-supplier transfer, purchase-after-certificate-expiry) hold as true invariants, not just service-layer validation. Includes the corresponding `semantic-audit.ts` ported rules for this domain. |
| Number of TCs | 21 |
