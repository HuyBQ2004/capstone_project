# Integration Test Case — Incident Investigation & Quarantine (MF11 Incident & Emergency Management)

---

## Feature: Incident Investigation & Quarantine

### 1. General Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Incident Investigation & Quarantine |
| Test requirement | Verify, against a real Testcontainers PostgreSQL instance, that the incident-investigation and quarantine flow is safe at the database level, not just at the service layer: the `incident_events` append-only ledger enforced by a DB `RULE` (ADR-M11-2), the one-way quarantine enforced by a DB `RULE` with no release-history growth (BR-217), race-safe concurrent severity assessment with exactly one `SEVERITY_SET` event, transactional-outbox semantics for the BR-148 emergency alert (event and severity write live/die together), cross-module writes going through read-only ports instead of direct `UPDATE`s on another module's tables (ADR-M11-7), the BR-151 traceability-map compilation SLA (`COMPILING → READY` within 4h, scoped to quarantined batches only), sample/lab-request linkage windows (`semantic-audit.ts` §4/§5), and the traceability-map "spine" that joins nodes across 4 modules by ID (not by name) via `ServingDishPort`/`DishQueryPort`, including the explicit blocked-test case if MF05 has not shipped `Dish.code` yet. |
| Number of TCs | 8 |
