# Integration Test Case — Incident Closure & CAPA Lifecycle (MF11 Incident & Emergency Management)

## Feature: Incident Closure & CAPA Lifecycle

### 1. General Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Incident Closure & CAPA Lifecycle |
| Test requirement | Verify, against a real Testcontainers PostgreSQL instance, that the incident-closure and CAPA (corrective-action) lifecycle is enforced at the database level, not just by the service: the closure gate (BR-155 + data-model constraint C1) rejects a premature `close` at the API layer while leaving DB state untouched; CAPA uniqueness and CHECK constraints (`uq_capa_one_per_incident`, `ck_capa_source`, `ck_capa_approved`, `ck_capa_evidence` — BR-216) reject invalid writes even when the service layer is bypassed via raw JDBC; the 2 `semantic-audit.ts` §9 CAPA rules (`CAPA_TRUOC_SU_CO` timing rule and `CAPA_GIAO_NCC_KHONG_TON_TAI` supplier-existence rule), including the named `"PTN-0007"` vs `"CAT-001"` regression case; and that 3 cross-module ports (M11-D08) are wired to real beans rather than silent stub beans, so that MF07's inspection-failure CAPA creation and idempotency actually function. |
| Number of TCs | 4 |
