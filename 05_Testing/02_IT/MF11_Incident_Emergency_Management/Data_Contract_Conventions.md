## Feature: Data Contract & 00_Common_Conventions Compliance

### 1. General Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Data Contract & 00_Common_Conventions Compliance |
| Test requirement | Verify MF11 complies with `00_Common_Conventions.md`: **D1** (JSON `id` is the business code, never the internal UUID, across all 5 resources and nested arrays); **D4** (every date field returns ISO-8601, with lenient dual-format parsing on input, overriding the stale `ADR-M11-5`/`§17.2 mục 12`); **D2/D6/D7** (3-field error envelope with array `details`, SCREAMING_SNAKE enums, English MSG text verbatim, and the `severity` NULL-in-DB ↔ `"PENDING"`-in-JSON display special case); **D9** (`BaseEntity` audit columns `created_by`/`updated_by` auto-filled via `AuditorAware`, distinct from business "who did it" UUID columns); and **D12** (derived/read-only fields and same-name-wrong-type fields sent by a naive client on write endpoints are silently ignored, DB stays unchanged — the "generic PATCH cannot bypass business gates" guard). |
| Number of TCs | 5 |
