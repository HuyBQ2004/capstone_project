### 1. General Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | System Security & Access Control |
| Test requirement | Verify the full 7-role × 28-endpoint authorization matrix (TDS §16); that data scope (`Own`/`Class`/`School`) is enforced at the service layer, not just role; cross-school tenant isolation; unauthenticated-request rejection (401 vs 403); PDPD (BR-173) protection against PII/health-data leakage via logs and storage keys; server-side enforcement of every business gate independent of the UI; and IDOR resistance for identity fields supplied in request bodies. |
